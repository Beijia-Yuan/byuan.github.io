About

Welcome! I am a PhD student working on integrating machine learning and physics to solve biological problems. Specifically, I'm very interested in the interactions of DNAs, RNAs and proteins. I spend my time exploring the journey from molecular code to a living creature—specifically, how DNA is physically packaged, how genes communicate and switch each other on and off, and how proteins intereact, and how all of these create the traits that drive evolution. My work also focuses on building models that are both quantitatively accurate and interpretable.

In my free time, I cook and read and sleep.
